## Clayvoyant 0.4.0: Sultan

The clay engine has a name: **Sultan**, after Ibrahim Sultan, who made Clayvoyant. This release gives it
bodies that never pass through anything, feet that never slide, and people who move like themselves.

### Solid, everywhere

- **Sitting is worked out, not assumed.** Every seat is measured from its own geometry where somebody
  sits on it, and each body sits there with its own legs: thighs on the seat, knees past its edge,
  feet on the floor in front of whatever is under it, a cushion that gives under the weight. The
  home office sofa no longer swallows legs (#28).
- **Getting to a seat and up again** the way people do: walk in standing, turn with small steps
  where there is room, step back one foot and then the other, and lower. A sofa and a bench seat two
  side by side.
- **Feet stay planted.** A foot on the floor never moves or turns until it lifts. A route with
  corners is one walk, carried on from where the feet are; a rising heel pivots on the ball of the
  foot; a turn on the spot steps round in pairs. Standing places clear the furniture and the other
  people.
- **Arms and hands stay outside the body.** Seated hands rest on the thighs, a hand on the face rests
  on that face, a carried mug or phone sits against the fingers, hands come off a laptop before it
  closes.
- **The set keeps out of itself.** Every loose thing rests on what is under it and keeps out of its
  neighbours: the cup on the cafe table no longer stands inside the laptop.
- **Checked before anything is drawn.** Every drawing of a shot is posed and measured first: against
  the set, the props, the other people and the body itself; what holds each body up; whether a
  standing foot slides. These are the measures motion research uses for physical plausibility,
  held to 5 mm. A shot that fails is not filmed, and the log says who, which part, in what and when.
  `sastudio check` does the same for a whole film without drawing anything.

### People who move like themselves

- `look.acting` takes a `manner` (calm, confident, nervous, lively, reserved, warm, stern) and an
  `age` (young, adult, older), and finer numbers for gaze, blinking, smiling, gestures, reaction time
  and stride. Longer legs take longer steps; an older body takes longer to sit down.
- Blinking follows what a person is doing: about 17 a minute at rest, 26 talking, 4.5 reading. A
  listener blinks as a line ends; a speaker looks away as a line begins and back before it ends.
- What a speaker's hands do follows how the line is said; listeners nod as far as they agree; a
  `laugh` moves the breath, the shoulders, the head and the face together.
- `sastudio validate` refuses staging a body cannot do, with the line and the fix.

[docs/RESEARCH.md](https://github.com/xIBBAx/clayvoyant/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH.md) credits the
studies and measures behind all of this.

### Sound that follows the picture

- **What happens is heard.** Footsteps where feet come down, on what they come down on; a body
  arriving on a seat or leaving it; a chair drawn in; a door's latch, swing and shut; a laptop's lid;
  hands typing. Read from the scene's own plan, so each sound lands on the drawing that shows it, and
  never because of a word in the dialogue. Cues for what no motion makes: a phone, a message, a knock.
- **Rooms with their own air.** Every effect rings in a room the size of its set, and every place has
  its room tone, never looped, crossing to the next place at a cut.
- **Music under the voices.** One file or many, per scene or for the whole film, matched in loudness,
  faded and crossfaded, dipping under speech without pumping.
- **Mastered and measured as heard:** -16 LUFS and -1.5 dBTP by default, checked again in the encoded
  film; the build warns when a target is not met.
- Bring your own recordings by kind, or `sastudio sounds install kenney` for real CC0 footsteps and
  doors. Changing only the sound films nothing again.
- [docs/SOUND.md](https://github.com/xIBBAx/clayvoyant/blob/main/docs/SOUND.md) explains every part,
  with what was measured.

### Fixed

- On Python 3.11 a film with real voices stopped at its first line, in every release since 0.1.0.
- A shot on one person is never filmed through somebody else, and never from behind somebody who has
  turned round more than half a circle.
- A walking foot never hovers over a floorboard a few millimetres lower than the one its heel left.

### Films

- **Nineteen Thirty** filmed again in English: Gopal sits on the sofa, not in it (#28). The Hindi films
  are on the 0.3.1 release, filmed before the fix.
- **Held Door**, a new sample whose sound is all made from what happens in it: footsteps, a door, a
  chair, typing, a laptop shut, a message, a phone that rings until it is answered, two rooms with
  their own air, and a theme under the voices.

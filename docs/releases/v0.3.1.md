## Clayvoyant 0.3.1

Films for India, ready to watch and to forward. Attached here, all made on one laptop by the code
in this release, with no account, no key and no video service:

- **Nineteen Thirty**, in English and Hindi: a retired teacher held on a "digital arrest" video call
  until his daughter walks in. Clay stop motion, 1280x720, from `examples/nineteen-thirty`.
- **Not So Fast**, in English and Hindi: the whole `india` template (a digital arrest, a UPI PIN,
  an OTP, an electricity-bill app, a fake helpline) with drawn characters, exactly as
  `sastudio init my-film --template india` makes it.
- Each of them as an upright **phone film** with large captions; Not So Fast also as **one phone clip
  per scam** (about a minute and 5 MB each, made to be forwarded); and the template's **posters**
  as a PDF in each language.

Every line of advice in them follows an advisory from I4C, the RBI or the Department of
Telecommunications; `docs/INDIA.md` links each one. Check them again before you show a film:
helplines and portals change.

### New

- **`sastudio share`** (`--each` for one clip per scene): the film laid out for a phone held upright (720x1280), with the
  organisation and title at the top, the film across the middle, each line's words below it in
  large type in its own script, and where to report along the foot, encoded under a size limit
  (15 MB by default). It also leaves a copy without subtitles in the picture, with its `.srt`.
- A film whose reporting address is still a placeholder now ends on its real reporting portal in
  large type instead (the `india` template ends on cybercrime.gov.in).
- `--version` prints the project's name.

### Files

- `Nineteen_Thirty.en-IN.*`, `Nineteen_Thirty.hi-IN.*`, `Not_So_Fast.en-IN.*`, `Not_So_Fast.hi-IN.*`:
  the films, their subtitles and their phone versions; `Not_So_Fast_<scene>.*.phone.mp4`, one clip per
  scam; `Not_So_Fast.posters.*.pdf`; `INDIA_SHA256SUMS`.
- `The_Fast_One.en-GB.*` and `SAMPLE_SHA256SUMS`: the clay sample film, as in 0.3.0.
- The wheel, the source archive and `SHA256SUMS`, built by the release workflow from the tag.
- The cards renderer's reference films and `MEDIA_SHA256SUMS` (see `docs/media/film/VERIFY.md`).

Install from the repository as in 0.3.0; this version is not on PyPI.

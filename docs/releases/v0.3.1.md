## Clayvoyant 0.3.1

Films for India, ready to watch and forward. Everything attached was made on one laptop by the code
in this release: no account, no API key, no video service.

### Films

- **Nineteen Thirty** (clay, about two minutes): a retired teacher held on a "digital arrest" video
  call until his daughter walks in. In English, Hindi, and Hindi in Roman letters.
- **Not So Fast** (drawn, about six minutes): the five scenes of the `india` template (digital
  arrest, a UPI PIN, an OTP, an electricity-bill app, a fake helpline) in the same three languages,
  also as one phone clip per scam and as printable posters.
- Each of these films also comes as an upright phone version with large captions.

Every line of advice follows an advisory from I4C, the RBI or the Department of
Telecommunications; `docs/INDIA.md` links each one. Helplines and portals change, so check them
before you show a film.

### New

- `sastudio share` lays a film out for a phone held upright (720x1280), under a size limit;
  `--each` makes one clip per scene.
- Hindi in Roman letters (`hi-Latn`): the Hindi film with its words written the way people type
  Hindi on a phone. Same voices, same shots; nothing is written twice.
- A film opens on its title in its own language (`project.title_translations`), and a film whose
  reporting address is still a placeholder ends on the real reporting portal instead.

### Known issue

In Nineteen Thirty, the seated character sinks into the sofa's cushion (#28).
